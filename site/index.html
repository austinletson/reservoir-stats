The graph needs a wider screen

A force layout of this many nodes is unreadable below about 700px. Use All packages for the same data as a sortable table.

Node area is proportional to how many packages depend on it; fill says what the package itself requires (hollow = requires nothing). Arrows point at what is required, so lower is more foundational. Dashed marks are groups of packages collapsed into one; click to open them. Click any package to centre the view on it. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Why one package at a time is the default. 82% of packages have nothing depending on them, the median package has none, and Mathlib alone accounts for 47% of every dependency edge. Drawn all at once that is roughly fifteen packages carrying structure and five hundred carrying none, which is a picture of one fact rather than a tool. Per-package the data is well behaved: 99% of packages have twelve or fewer neighbours.
